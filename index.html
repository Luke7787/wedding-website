How We Met

4.1.23

Sometimes, you meet someone without realizing just how important they’re going to become. We first crossed paths in 2023, and what started as a simple introduction slowly turned into something much more meaningful.

As we got to know each other, we understood each other more and more. We supported each other through whatever life brought our way, and before we knew it, we had made countless memories together and found something truly special in one another.

After talking for a little while, I decided to ask her out on April Fools’ Day. I figured it would be a funny date to remember. To my delight, she said yes. Little did we know that a simple April Fools’ Day joke would be the beginning of everything that brought us here today.

The Proposal

4.4.26

For our 3rd year anniversary of dating, I had Gizelle come up to San Francisco to celebrate with me. I told her we were going to celebrate our anniversary, so I had us wear matching outfits and even had her get her makeup and nails done. Little did she know, all of that was just my cover up for the proposal. I figured it was the perfect time to do it because she would least expect it.

I told her that I wanted to watch the sunset, and thankfully, she agreed lol. I took her to Baker Beach in San Francisco, and although it was packed, we were fortunate enough to find a small section available. I told her that we should go down to the water to take some cute photos, and from there, at the right moment, I got down on one knee and asked her if she would be blessed to make me food and wash my clothes for the rest of my life.

Thankfully, she said yes willingly and happily. 🤍

Absolutely! We’d love to celebrate with the whole family. Children are welcome to join us, as long as they are well supervised throughout the celebration.

Things To Do

Avila Beach

San Luis Obispo County, California

A laid-back beach town with a wide sandy beach, a walkable waterfront, and local shops and restaurants just steps from the ocean. An easygoing spot to slow down and enjoy the Central Coast.

Directions

Morro Bay

Morro Bay, California

An iconic coastal town with a lively harbor and a relaxed waterfront atmosphere. Known for its fishing boats, local shops, and oceanfront scenery.

Directions

Hearst Castle

San Luis Obispo County, California

An iconic castle with breathtaking views, beautiful Spanish and Mediterranean architecture. Built by William Randolph Hearst, it’s filled with European art, elaborate rooms, and intricate details throughout.

Directions

Ravine Waterpark

Paso Robles, California

A lively water park with exciting slides, a wave pool, lazy river, and splash areas for all ages. A fun place to cool off and spend the day in Paso Robles.
